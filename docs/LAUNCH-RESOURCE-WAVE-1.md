# Launch resource catalog — Wave 1 candidates

Every entry below must be reverified from its source immediately before database publication. These are research candidates, not ShelterPawtners partnerships.

## Consumer/adopter-facing priority

1. PetSmart Adoption Kit — adopter coupon/savings kit; current PetSmart policy still references Adoption Kit coupons.
2. Adopt a Pet Shelter Plus — participating-shelter adopters receive guidance and special discounts.
3. PetPartners 30-Day Pet Insurance Coverage — newly adopted dogs/cats with a participating shelter/rescue/vet activation code may receive 30 days of coverage; eligibility applies.
4. Trupanion shelter/rescue adoption coverage — participating shelter/rescue adopter coverage program; verify exact current terms before listing.
5. 24Petwatch Shelter Adoption Program — participating shelter adoption insurance trial; payment/renewal terms need prominent disclosure.
6. Banfield SAVD adopter benefit — adopters from participating SAVD shelters/rescues may receive a Free First Exam Coupon; the shelter's 25% discount is not an adopter discount.
7. BISSELL Pet Foundation Empty the Shelters — reduced adoption fees at participating shelters during current event windows; Fall 2026 event September 18–30.
8. Zoetis + Adopt a Pet Pet Adopter Starter Pack — free adopter resource pack; list as a community resource unless a monetary offer is independently verified.

## Shelter/rescue-facing priority

9. Petfinder Feeding Partners / Purina Pro Plan — qualified Petfinder Pro shelters/rescues can save up to 50% on select products and receive adopter-support materials.
10. Hill's Food, Shelter & Love — shelter nutrition support/discount program; any seasonal adopter kit must be separately date-verified.
11. GoodPup shelter programs — adopter training benefits through participating shelters; exact current adopter terms require fresh confirmation.
12. MetLife Pet shelter/rescue programs — insurer works with shelters/rescues; publish only an exact adopter benefit supported by a current participating-program source.
13. Petco Love adoption network/resources — strong adoption-resource ecosystem; do not revive expired 2025 coupon values as current 2026 discounts.

## Expansion queue

Research current programs from:

- IAMS / PEDIGREE / Nutro / Royal Canin / Greenies / Temptations / PrettyLitter / Whistle;
- Chewy and other national retailers;
- VCA, Thrive, Vetco and other veterinary networks;
- Rover, Wag, training/grooming/daycare networks;
- microchip/GPS/pet-tech providers;
- nationwide pet insurance brands;
- Michigan and Detroit-area businesses with explicit newly adopted/rescue pet benefits.

The master list is intentionally open-ended because programs are shelter-dependent, regional, seasonal, and frequently changed. Publication requires current evidence, not merely historical mentions.
