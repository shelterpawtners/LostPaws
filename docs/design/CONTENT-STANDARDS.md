# Content standards

## Writing quality

Customer-facing copy requires correct spelling and grammar, clear sentence structure, natural language, concise explanations, strong information hierarchy, professional editing, consistent terminology, clear calls to action, and accurate claims.

Avoid em dashes, unnecessary dashed sentence constructions, generic AI phrasing, excessive fragments, fake inspirational language, corporate filler, buzzwords, repeated three-item slogans used merely for rhythm, unsubstantiated claims, excessive exclamation marks, and overly cute pet language. Normal grammatical hyphenation is acceptable when it improves clarity.

## Claims and trust

- Describe planned capabilities as planned until implemented and verified. Calls to action must not imply unavailable functionality.
- Do not fabricate statistics, social proof, testimonials, discounts, partnerships, research findings, or impact results.
- Never imply formal affiliation with Lost Lands Music Festival, Excision, another festival, artist, promoter, or vendor without an established formal relationship.
- Treat lifetime savings access as a long-term goal until actual program terms support a stronger claim. Distinguish estimated savings from confirmed monetary outcomes.
- Passport information must not imply that ShelterPawtners is a veterinarian or medical authority. Clearly distinguish contributed records from platform-generated information.
- Support factual marketing claims with traceable sources and applicable dates or context. Research-informed hooks must be relevant and non-manipulative; avoid false urgency, guilt, or hidden conditions.

## Page editing workflow

Start with the user's need and a clear page purpose. Write a strong opening hook, then order supporting information by importance. Explain what the visitor receives and how participation supports shelter pets. Make the next action specific, with eligibility or other material conditions close to the claim.

Before publication, edit for accuracy, spelling, grammar, terminology, readability, punctuation, and actual feature availability. Check headings and calls to action in the rendered layout. Remove em dashes from customer-facing examples and final copy.

## Illustrative copy, not published claims

The following is concept-stage language only. It does not assert a working feature or active partner program:

> ShelterPawtners is building a community that helps people care for their pets and support shelter adoption.

> LostPaws brings music communities into the shelter adoption mission through practical participation.

Do not publish feature-specific action labels until their destinations function. Final copy should reflect the approved page purpose and actual release scope.
