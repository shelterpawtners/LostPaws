# Brand design system

## Status

This document preserves the established ShelterPawtners brand direction. ShelterPawtners black-text, white-text, and icon-only PNG assets and a LostPaws PNG asset were supplied and visually reviewed on September 5, 2026. They have not yet been committed as production assets. Permanent hex values, final typography, vector masters, and asset-specific visual tokens are not established. Derive final colors from the actual ShelterPawtners logo assets when available, with accessible functional variants where necessary.

## Visual character

The experience should feel modern, premium, forward thinking, warm, hopeful, credible, emotionally engaging, technology enabled, and community driven. Favor real pet photography over cartoon illustrations. Use imagery with appropriate rights and avoid suggesting that stock photography depicts a real participating shelter, guardian, or partner.

Avoid cartoon animals, excessive paw prints, childish illustration, cheap nonprofit templates, purposeless AI-style gradients, excessive glassmorphism, visual clutter, clip art, and emoji navigation icons.

## Color and visual system

The established iridescent family includes purple, violet, blue, and teal, with subtle pink accents where appropriate. Use gradients intentionally to support hierarchy and brand expression. Do not rely on them to carry text legibility or meaning alone.

After logo review, document source assets and semantic tokens for text, surfaces, borders, actions, focus, and status. Select reusable typography, spacing, component states, and responsive conventions during approved design work. Do not invent permanent brand hex values before reviewing the logo. Any temporary exploration must be explicitly labeled provisional.

## Accessibility and responsiveness

Support WCAG-conscious contrast, readable typography, visible focus, keyboard navigation, and responsive layout. Choose and verify the formal conformance target before implementation; this document does not claim certification.

Future reviews should include text over imagery and gradients, color-independent status cues, meaningful image alternatives, form labels and error messages, semantic headings, logical keyboard order, usable zoom, and narrow-screen layouts. Components must account for loading, empty, error, disabled, and success states without obscuring important information.

## Required design sequence

1. Establish the product requirement.
2. Identify the user need.
3. Define the page purpose.
4. Develop information architecture.
5. Establish content hierarchy.
6. Create a wireframe or layout concept.
7. Apply design system components.
8. Define responsive behavior.
9. Review accessibility.
10. Implement after authorization.
11. Review the real page in a browser.
12. Iterate on the observed result.

Do not jump from requirements directly to finished pages. A compiling page is not a completed design. Reuse coherent components while keeping the primary audiences and purpose of each page visible.

## RAVE Shelter brand

RAVE Shelter means **Rewarding Adoption with Vendor Exclusives**.

Approved tagline (2026-09-12 owner direction, supersedes the earlier "Deals for ravers" tagline):

> Rave with purpose. Shop with impact.

RAVE Shelter should bridge the ShelterPawtners iridescent purple, blue, and teal palette with the bolder music-community energy of LostPaws. It should feel mature, premium, energetic, and credible. Use original sound, connection, heart, shelter, exchange, or event geometry rather than cartoon animals or generic paw-print clip art.

The complete lockup should support:

- full logo with RAVE Shelter, expansion, and tagline;
- simplified horizontal website header;
- compact marketplace and social mark;
- one-color and high-contrast variants;
- transparent-background production files;
- legibility on printed festival signup cards.

Do not mimic the identity of Lost Lands, Excision, another festival, artist, promoter, or vendor.

## Homepage application

The homepage should visually explain mutual ecosystem value and the collective effect of many small actions. Prioritize a clear opening, understandable benefits, and a next step over decorative density or a long business explanation. Use [Product requirements](PRODUCT-REQUIREMENTS.md) for page outcomes and [Content standards](CONTENT-STANDARDS.md) for hooks and copy. LostPaws may express its music-community context without mimicking an unaffiliated festival's identity or implying endorsement.
