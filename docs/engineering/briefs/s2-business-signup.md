# S2 - Fast business signup + partner profile

Follow docs/engineering/briefs/mobile-forms-standard.md. Own: src/main.tsx functions `Signup` and `PartnerOrganizationOnboarding` (and their helpers only), src/components/PartnerProfileEditor.tsx, new src/components/partner-onboarding.css. Do not edit other functions in main.tsx.
Goal: a RAVE vendor or pet business signs up in under a minute with the bare basics: email/password (existing flow) + company name (+ one-tap choice of business type), then lands on "Make your first offer" (/partner/offers, with ?channel=rave for RAVE vendors). All other profile fields (address, hours, links, logo, description, categories...) move behind "Finish your profile later" with a visible progress checklist on the offers page/dashboard entry point you control. Make required fields the minimum the server needs (inspect the RPC/insert used; if the server needs more, default honestly or defer to first publish; do not change DB). Single column, sticky continue button. PartnerProfileEditor: single column, sectioned accordions, mobile-friendly logo/image upload, no side-by-side fields below 1024px.
