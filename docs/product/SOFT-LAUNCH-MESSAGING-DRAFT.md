# Soft Launch Messaging Draft

Status: owner-review draft. This is messaging source material, not approved final public copy.

## Core ShelterPawtners message

**Adopt. Save for life. Support shelters.**

ShelterPawtners is designed to reward people who adopt from animal shelters with a Certified Shelter Pet Passport that can unlock lifetime savings from participating businesses.

The goal is simple: make pet ownership more affordable, help small businesses find loyal customers, and create new ways to support shelters through the purchasing power of the community.

Pet Guardian accounts are free. The product is designed to lower pet ownership costs, never add another subscription cost to pet owners.

## Core RAVE Shelter message

# Rave with purpose. Shop with impact.

**RAVE Shelter — Rewarding Adoption with Vendor Exclusives**

RAVE Shelter connects ravers with festival vendors, indie creators, makers, and small businesses offering useful deals and experiences while helping build new pathways of support for animal shelters.

We want to help participating businesses make a great first impression, reach new customers, and create repeat relationships. When those businesses choose to support shelters with part of the margin generated through participating sales, the community's spending can become something much bigger than a one-time purchase.

## Immediate soft-launch CTA

**RIGHT NOW, WE NEED YOU TO SIGN UP AND SHOW DEMAND.**

The marketplace gets stronger when more Guardians, vendors, creators, businesses, and shelters raise their hands early.

Every registration helps prove that people want a marketplace where adoption is rewarded, small businesses are supported, and shelter impact can be tied to real purchasing activity.

## RAVE Shelter Hero Vendor draft

### Sell more. Give more. Get recognized for it.

RAVE Shelter is free to join. We want to help creators, festival vendors, artists, makers, and small businesses reach people already interested in what they sell.

Businesses that commit 5% or more of eligible participating sales to shelter support can qualify for **RAVE Shelter Hero Vendor** recognition.

Hero Vendors should receive visible recognition, including enhanced placement, Hero badges, dedicated discovery filters, featured opportunities, and impact visibility when the reporting system supports it.

The idea is a growth flywheel:

**more discovery -> more sales -> more eligible support -> more shelter impact -> stronger customer loyalty -> more sales.**

## Shelter impact story

This is a passion project, a technology-for-good project, and a personal project.

The system is being built around a simple belief: nobody should lose a pet because care became unaffordable, and no dog or cat should lose its chance simply because a shelter ran out of room or resources.

ShelterPawtners cannot guarantee those outcomes, but it can build new economic connections between adopters, customers, small businesses, and shelters that create more opportunities for help than isolated action alone.

## Transparency message

People should be able to understand where support is going.

The product direction is to let users choose an eligible shelter where supported by the final transaction model, see realized activity, and distinguish projected commitments from completed money movement.

Do not publish tax-deductibility promises or automated tax-form promises until the underlying financial structure and reporting workflow are implemented and reviewed.
