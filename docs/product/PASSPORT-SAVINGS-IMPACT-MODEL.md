# Certified Shelter Pet Passport — Savings & Impact Model

Status: **FRAMEWORK ONLY — assumptions and public figures not yet approved**  
Date: 2026-09-12

## Purpose

Quantify the potential consumer savings, business value, and shelter-support impact created by the Certified Shelter Pet Passport without presenting speculative projections as realized results.

This model will support:

- ShelterPawtners marketplace education;
- RAVE Shelter storytelling;
- Guardian conversion;
- vendor recruitment;
- shelter recruitment;
- investor/sponsor materials;
- future dashboards.

## Required scenarios

Calculate at minimum:

- first month;
- first year;
- 15-year pet lifetime;
- low / base / high utilization.

## Separate value buckets

Never collapse these into one number:

1. **Guardian savings** — discounts actually available/used.
2. **Participating business revenue** — customer sales attributable to the marketplace where measurable.
3. **Business-funded shelter support** — qualifying contributions tied to participating sales.
4. **Guardian-directed support** — optional user-directed support only where the actual money-movement workflow supports it.
5. **Projected impact** — illustrative scenarios based on explicit assumptions.
6. **Realized impact** — completed/verified transactions only.

## Inputs to research/approve

### Household/pet spending

Create reasonable category baskets for a dog/cat household, for example:

- food;
- treats;
- toys;
- collars/leashes/harnesses;
- grooming;
- training;
- boarding/daycare;
- routine wellness/services;
- accessories;
- event/travel-related pet spending where relevant.

Do not publish averages without a cited source and date.

### Discount assumptions

For each category define:

- expected participating discount;
- frequency of eligible purchases;
- likely utilization rate;
- exclusions/limits;
- whether offer is one-time, recurring, or lifetime/repeat eligible.

### Pet lifetime

Use explicit lifespan assumptions by scenario. The owner specifically wants a 15-year illustrative lifetime view; it must be labeled as a scenario, not a universal pet lifespan claim.

### Business contribution assumptions

Model contribution rates separately, including the proposed **RAVE Shelter Hero Vendor 5%+** threshold.

Do not assume every marketplace vendor contributes 5%.

### Scale scenarios

After per-household math is stable, model aggregate scenarios such as:

- 1,000 participating Guardians/households;
- 10,000;
- 100,000;
- 1,000,000;

Where transaction volume is a better denominator, also model by annual eligible sales volume.

## Output format

Every published scenario should show:

- assumptions;
- formula;
- low/base/high result;
- what is included/excluded;
- whether result is projected or realized;
- source/date for externally researched inputs.

## Public-story objective

The user should be able to understand:

> A small discount on one purchase is useful. Repeated across years of pet ownership and thousands of households, coordinated savings and business participation can become a meaningful economic system for adopters, small businesses, and shelters.

The model must show this potential without overstating certainty.

## Guardrails

- no invented statistics;
- no guaranteed savings claims;
- no claim that projected support has been donated;
- no tax-deductibility claims without validated transaction structure;
- no implication that every participating vendor is a Hero Vendor;
- clearly separate consumer savings from shelter support;
- clearly separate vendor-funded support from optional Guardian-directed support.
