# Owner Decisions — Next Marketplace / RAVE Shelter Sprint

Status: open owner review list.

## Already directed by owner

- RAVE Shelter consumer expansion = **Rewarding Adoption with Vendor Exclusives**.
- Primary RAVE Shelter line = **Rave with purpose. Shop with impact.**
- LostPaws is reserved for Lost Lands and other Excision-related festivals/headlining shows owned/run/majority-associated with Excision.
- Standard pet businesses should not be shown rave/festival content unless they opt into that audience.
- Events should be a shared cross-audience model with pet/human/both classification and business participation roles.
- Pet Guardians remain free.
- Founding business partners registering before 2027 should retain a free founding account and not be charged a future subscription fee for that account.
- RAVE Shelter Hero Vendor should be a major recognition tier for vendors committing 5%+ of eligible participating sales to shelter support.
- Explainer tiles should use professional branded visuals, not emoji.
- Marketplace value should eventually show illustrative 1-month, 1-year, and 15-year savings/impact scenarios backed by explicit assumptions.

## Decisions to finalize before public wording / financial implementation

1. **Founding free-account guarantee**
   - Is the promise tied to the legal business, account, owner, or organization record?
   - Is it transferable if a business is sold?
   - Are optional paid add-ons allowed later while core account access remains free?
   - What abuse/duplicate-account controls apply?

2. **Hero Vendor 5% basis**
   - 5% of gross eligible sale?
   - 5% after discounts?
   - before/after refunds, sales tax, shipping, processor fees?
   - only sales attributed through ShelterPawtners/RAVE Shelter?

3. **Shelter recipient eligibility**
   - verified 501(c)(3) only at launch?
   - municipal shelters?
   - rescues/fiscal sponsors?
   - how recipient eligibility is verified.

4. **Money movement**
   - vendor pays shelter directly vs platform-facilitated transfer;
   - timing and minimums;
   - refunds/chargebacks;
   - reporting evidence.

5. **Tax reporting**
   - what annual summary is informational vs an actual tax document;
   - who is donor of record;
   - whether ShelterPawtners ever takes custody of funds.

6. **Certified Shelter Pet Passport naming/verification**
   - final public criteria for use of `Certified`;
   - whether certification means shelter-confirmed adoption only or additional validation.

These open decisions must not block Track 1 mobile/branding/conversion work, but they should block irreversible financial automation and unsupported public tax claims.
