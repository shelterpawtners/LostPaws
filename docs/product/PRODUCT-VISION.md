# Product vision

## Mission

ShelterPawtners exists to increase successful shelter pet adoptions and improve long-term outcomes for adopted pets. It connects shelters, guardians, pet businesses, service providers, community partners, and supporters through practical benefits, better information, and measurable participation.

Small contributions from many participants can create meaningful benefits for shelter pets while every participant receives value. The business should be framed as an ecosystem where everyone can win. It is not solely a discount marketplace or a traditional donation program.

## Connected value areas

| Area      | Intended value                                                                                                                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Care      | Pet identity and history, continuity across adoption, relevant information shared with authorized caregivers, and useful guardian support. |
| Savings   | Practical offers and support that can reduce the cost of caring for pets, with eligibility and terms made clear.                           |
| Community | Shelters, businesses, providers, and wider communities contributing in practical, measurable ways.                                         |

Data and intelligence support these areas rather than replace them. The long-term platform combines identity, pet history, savings, partner participation, shelter validation, community support, and insights.

## Ecosystem value

| Participant                                         | Value the platform should create                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Guardians and prospective adopters                  | Useful pet information, savings, support, and easier continuity of care.                                                |
| Shelters and rescues                                | Adoption support, stronger post-adoption relationships, improved records, community participation, and future insights. |
| Pet businesses                                      | Customer acquisition, community visibility, mission participation, and measurable engagement.                           |
| Veterinarians and service providers                 | Relevant pet history when authorized and stronger guardian relationships.                                               |
| Sponsors, brands, event vendors, community partners | Practical animal welfare participation without requiring traditional charitable donations.                              |
| Shelter pets                                        | Better support before, during, and after adoption.                                                                      |

These are intended outcomes. Do not present them as demonstrated results without evidence.

## Audience priority

Design for these audiences in order:

1. Pet guardians and prospective adopters.
2. Shelters and rescues.
3. Pet businesses and service partners.
4. Veterinarians and other authorized providers.
5. Sponsors, brands, event partners, and community organizations.
6. Researchers, animal welfare leaders, and executives as later audiences.

Current pets need not come from a shelter to receive a Digital Pet Passport. Some benefits may require verified shelter adoption. Immediate value should extend to existing guardians as well as new adopters.

## RAVE Shelter and LostPaws within ShelterPawtners

Status: this section reflects the 2026-09-12 owner-directed correction in `docs/product/DUAL-MARKETPLACE-RAVE-SHELTER-EXECUTION-PLAN.md`, which supersedes the earlier "LostPaws contains RAVE Shelter" framing.

**RAVE Shelter** means **Rewarding Adoption with Vendor Exclusives**. Approved tagline: **Rave with purpose. Shop with impact.** RAVE Shelter is the human/festival-facing marketplace and community program powered by ShelterPawtners — it connects ravers, festival-goers, vendors, artists, brands, and community members with the shelter adoption mission. Its offers may be human, music, apparel, art, food, wellness, festival, or other attendee-focused products and services, using the values commonly described as peace, love, unity, and respect to support the ShelterPawtners mission.

**LostPaws** is a distinct RAVE Shelter community initiative, not the other way around, and the name of this application repository. LostPaws is reserved specifically for Lost Lands and other Excision-related festivals/headlining shows owned, run, or majority-associated with Excision. Other festivals and events get their own RAVE Shelter activations (for example "RAVE Shelter — Electric Forest Activation") rather than the LostPaws name.

Possible participation includes deeper discounts, special products, purchase-linked donations, adoption incentives, or other benefits. These are possibilities, not existing offers or commitments.

No official affiliation with Lost Lands Music Festival, Excision, any other festival, artist, promoter, or vendor may be implied without a formal relationship.

## Experience intent

Visitors should quickly understand what ShelterPawtners is, why it matters to them, what value they can receive, how participation helps shelter pets, and what to do next. Make the collective value of many small actions understandable without a long business explanation.
