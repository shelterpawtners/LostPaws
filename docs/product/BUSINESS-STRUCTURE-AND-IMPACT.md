# Business structure and impact

ShelterPawtners is currently a startup initiative. Records must not imply approved nonprofit, B Corp, fiscal-sponsor, charitable-recipient, or official festival status unless separately verified.

Organizations are CRM-style accounts with expandable types, locations, memberships, parent/child hierarchy, and typed relationships. Operational verification and charitable eligibility are separate. Sibling organizations may be inferred from a shared parent; explicit records capture affiliation, sponsorship, preferred-vendor, management, and funding relationships.

Impact reporting will be derived from finalized economic events, verified redemptions, settled donations, and canonical shelter lifecycle events. Demo rows are excluded. Adoption, savings, and giving figures retain provenance and are not inflated by intentions, claims, or unverified statements.
