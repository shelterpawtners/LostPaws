# DRAFT — Terms of Service

**Status: owner/legal review required. Not approved for publication.**

Last draft update: 2026-09-10

These draft Terms are a working product document for ShelterPawtners / RAVE Shelter and are not final legal advice.

## 1. Service

ShelterPawtners provides digital tools intended to support adopted pets, Guardians, shelters/rescues, participating PetBiz partners, and related community experiences. Features may include Digital Pet Passports, adoption verification, offers/discounts, savings tracking, provider notes, community features, and related services.

## 2. Accounts and eligibility

Users must provide accurate information, protect account credentials, and use the service only for lawful purposes. Organization representatives must have authority to act for the organization they register or manage. The service may impose additional eligibility or verification requirements for specific features.

## 3. Pet and adoption information

Pet records and adoption-verification information may be supplied by Guardians, shelters/rescues, providers, or other authorized participants. ShelterPawtners may facilitate verification but does not guarantee that every submitted fact is complete or independently verified unless the product explicitly labels it as verified.

## 4. Digital Pet Passport

The Digital Pet Passport is an informational record and is not a substitute for veterinary records, professional veterinary advice, licensing, microchip registration, or legally required ownership/adoption documentation. Users are responsible for determining which records they must maintain with third parties or government agencies.

## 5. Offers, discounts, and Marketplace

Participating PetBiz partners control the terms, inventory, eligibility, expiration, and fulfillment of their offers unless ShelterPawtners explicitly states otherwise. Display of an offer does not guarantee availability. Savings totals may be informational estimates or transaction records depending on the feature and should not be treated as cash, stored value, or a financial account.

No customer-facing rule concerning “verified savings,” donation settlement, or financial allocation should be inferred from this draft; those policies require separate owner approval.

## 6. Donations and shelter support

Where the service displays or facilitates shelter-support amounts, the applicable program terms, timing, calculation, and settlement rules must be separately disclosed before money movement occurs. This draft does not authorize or define OD-004 settlement/donation rules.

## 7. User content and permissions

Users retain ownership of content they submit, subject to the permissions reasonably required for ShelterPawtners to host, process, display, secure, back up, and transmit that content to provide requested features. Users must have the right to submit the information and media they upload.

## 8. Acceptable use

Users may not misuse the service, attempt unauthorized access, interfere with security controls, impersonate others, upload unlawful or harmful content, scrape or exploit personal information contrary to law or product controls, or use the platform to facilitate fraud or animal abuse.

## 9. Third-party services

ShelterPawtners is not responsible for third-party services outside its control except as required by law or contract.

## 10. Availability and changes

Features may change, be suspended, or be discontinued. ShelterPawtners may perform maintenance, correct errors, enforce security requirements, and modify eligibility or verification workflows to protect users and the service.

## 11. Disclaimers
