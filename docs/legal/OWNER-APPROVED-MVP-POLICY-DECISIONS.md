## Identity, eligibility, and disputes

1. **Legal/public name:** Use **Shelter Pawtners** as the public-facing contracting/business name for MVP, subject to matching the exact registered entity name before final publication.
2. **Mailing address:** Use **1627 E. 6th Street, Royal Oak, MI 48067** temporarily for legal notices. Replace later with a commercial/business mailing address when practical.
3. **Launch geography:** United States only for MVP.
4. **Minimum age:** Individual account holders must be 18 or older. Do not support independent minor accounts or parental-consent flows at MVP.
5. **Governing law:** Michigan law.
6. **Preferred dispute venue:** Oakland County, Michigan, subject to non-waivable consumer-law rights.
7. **Mandatory arbitration:** No for MVP.
8. **Class-action waiver:** No for MVP.
9. **Privacy/legal contact:** Use **privacy@shelterpawtners.com** as the primary privacy/legal contact. A separate legal inbox may be added later if useful.

## Marketplace, savings, and shelter support

10. **Marketplace responsibility:** PetBiz/RAVE providers are responsible for their own offer terms, inventory, availability, exclusions, expiration, fulfillment, taxes/fees, products/services, and customer-service disputes. Shelter Pawtners may remove misleading or stale listings but does not guarantee provider performance, sales, traffic, availability, or official event placement.
11. **Savings language:** MVP savings are informational estimates or recorded offer values only. Do not call them “verified savings” until OD-003 is formally resolved.
12. **Savings are not money:** Displayed savings are not cash, stored value, a financial account, or a guaranteed benefit.
13. **Shelter-support/donation handling:** MVP does not claim that Shelter Pawtners collects, holds, transfers, settles, or guarantees charitable funds. Any future money movement requires separate disclosed rules before launch.

## Cookies, storage, analytics, and privacy model

14. **Essential cookies/storage only:** Use cookies/local/session storage only as needed for authentication, security, app state, and preferences unless additional tracking is deliberately introduced later.
15. **Advertising tracking:** Do not use personal information for third-party advertising in MVP.
16. **Sale of personal information:** Shelter Pawtners will not sell personal information in MVP.
17. **Analytics:** Allow operational logs and aggregate/de-identified product analytics for security, reliability, and product improvement. Do not claim use of a named analytics provider unless one is actually enabled.
18. **Retention philosophy:** Use purpose-based retention. Keep data only as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, security, fraud prevention, disputes, legal obligations, and legitimate pet-history continuity.
19. **Account/profile deletion:** On a verified deletion request, remove or anonymize account/profile information where practical, subject to legal, security, fraud, and dispute-related exceptions.
20. **Pet Passport retention:** Keep Passport data while the service/Passport is active. Allow Guardian-controlled deletion where appropriate while preserving limited records when legitimately needed for history, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.
21. **Adoption-verification records:** Retain adoption-verification records long enough to preserve verification integrity and handle disputes. Do not promise immediate deletion of audit/verification records.
22. **Support-record target:** Internal target of roughly 2–3 years unless a dispute, security matter, or legal requirement justifies longer retention.
23. **Security/audit-log target:** Internal target of roughly 12–24 months, adjustable if security needs require it.
24. **Backups:** Deleted information may remain temporarily in backups until normal backup expiration. Do not promise instant deletion from every backup copy.
25. **Privacy rights:** Support verified requests for access, correction, deletion, export, and account closure.
26. **Identity verification:** Verify requester identity before providing, changing, exporting, or deleting personal information.
27. **Response deadlines:** Respond within the time required by applicable law rather than promising one universal deadline.
28. **Deletion exceptions:** Permit limited retention where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal requirements, dispute resolution, or legitimate pet/adoption-history integrity.

## Pet/adoption boundaries and support/safety

29. **Digital Pet Passport boundary:** The Digital Pet Passport is an informational record, not a substitute for veterinary records, veterinary advice, government licensing, microchip registration, or legal ownership/adoption documents.
30. **Adoption-verification boundary:** Adoption verification confirms only the limited adoption fact/relationship checked by the workflow. It does not independently establish legal title, veterinary authority, microchip ownership, or government registration.
31. **Guardian-controlled privacy:** Private Passport/account information remains private by default unless the Guardian deliberately shares it or a clearly disclosed workflow requires access.
32. **Support-data privacy:** Support tickets, diagnostic information, and customer communications are private operational data and must not automatically be published to GitHub or other public systems.
33. **Animal safety/abuse:** Shelter Pawtners may investigate, restrict, suspend, or remove accounts/content associated with suspected fraud, abuse, threats, animal mistreatment, or unlawful activity and may report matters where required by law or reasonably necessary for safety.
34. **AI-use disclosure:** Shelter Pawtners may use automated tools, including AI, for support, content organization, security/fraud review, and operations. Do not represent AI as independently making veterinary, legal, ownership, or adoption determinations.

## Items to revisit after MVP / scale triggers

Revisit this policy set when any of the following occurs:

- **Entity formation/change:** exact registered LLC/nonprofit/corporate name changes, EIN/entity structure changes, or a nonprofit becomes the contracting/controller entity.
- **Address/privacy:** a commercial mailbox, registered office, or counsel address replaces the temporary home address.
- **International expansion:** users outside the U.S. are intentionally supported; assess GDPR/UK GDPR/Canada and cross-border transfer requirements before launch there.
- **Minors:** any product is intentionally offered to users under 18; design parental-consent/age-assurance and children’s privacy controls first.
- **Payments/donations:** OD-004 or any settlement, donation, payment, stored-value, payout, fundraising, or tax-receipt capability is introduced.
- **Verified savings:** OD-003 is resolved and public claims move from informational savings to verified/audited calculations.
- **Advertising/marketing:** targeted advertising, ad-network tracking, retargeting pixels, data-sharing for ads, or marketing automation is introduced.
- **New analytics/telemetry:** a named analytics, session-replay, error-monitoring, mapping, geolocation, or similar provider begins receiving production data.
- **Meta/Facebook:** Facebook Login or Meta integrations are enabled; update provider inventory, privacy text, review package, and post-callback acceptance.
- **New processors/integrations:** veterinary, shelter, PetBiz, payments, CRM, or other third-party connectors begin processing personal data.
- **Sensitive/medical data growth:** veterinary/medical documents, detailed health records, precise location, or other sensitive data become materially more central to the product.
- **Data volume/security maturity:** user count, transaction volume, security incidents, support burden, or contractual obligations justify shorter/longer retention, dedicated DPA/security terms, incident-response SLAs, or formal compliance programs.
- **Enterprise/B2B contracts:** shelters, PetBiz partners, sponsors, or providers require separate commercial terms, DPAs, SLAs, indemnities, insurance, or audit rights.
- **Marketplace economics:** commissions, fees, paid placement, subscriptions, sponsored listings, or provider billing are introduced.
- **Geographic/regulatory thresholds:** company size, revenue, consumer counts, or data practices trigger additional U.S. state privacy statutes or other regulatory obligations.
- **Dispute strategy:** business scale/risk justifies reconsidering arbitration, class-action waiver, liability cap formula, or persona-specific indemnity with counsel.
- **AI scope:** automated tools begin making or materially influencing consequential decisions; reassess disclosure, human-review, recordkeeping, bias/safety, and appeal controls.
- **Pet Passport portability/public sharing:** public/emergency sharing, interoperability, external export/import, ownership transfer, or longitudinal health record features expand materially.
