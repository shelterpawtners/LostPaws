# Seven Star Shelters — landing page brief

Status: owner-approved concept for parallel build.

## Purpose

Create the next RAVE Shelter festival activation after LostPaws:

**Seven Star Shelters — Spread Shelter Love**

This is an independent RAVE Shelter community activation designed for people attending or following Seven Stars and the wider GRiZ community. It should amplify the community values already present in that ecosystem while connecting ravers, vendors, creators, and shelter supporters to the ShelterPawtners mission.

Do **not** imply that ShelterPawtners, RAVE Shelter, or Seven Star Shelters is officially affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by GRiZ, Seven Stars, 100x, promoters, venues, or charitable partners unless that relationship is later documented.

## Core message

**SHOW LOVE. SPREAD SHELTER LOVE.**

Supporting line:

> A RAVE Shelter mission installment inspired by community, generosity, and the belief that music can help create a kinder world for people and pets.

Primary objective:

1. grow early awareness and registrations;
2. recruit RAVE Shelter vendors before the festival;
3. connect attendees with festival-relevant offers;
4. direct visitors toward legitimate GRiZ / community-giving resources as an amplification layer;
5. educate visitors about the ShelterPawtners mission without competing with GRiZ's existing causes.

## Research-grounded community alignment

### Seven Stars

Official Seven Stars materials describe the festival as three days of music, community, and adventure and publish **Seven Pillars** that frame the community culture around Inclusion, Sovereign Self, Sharing, Leave No Trace, Curiosity, Kula (Unified Heart), and Bunga (Wild Abandon).

Official links:

- Seven Stars: https://www.sevenstarsfest.com/
- Seven Pillars: https://www.sevenstarsfest.com/seven-pillars
- Seven Stars lineup: https://www.sevenstarsfest.com/lineup

### GRiZ / Show Love, Spread Love

GRiZ's public brand and community history are strongly associated with **Show Love, Spread Love** and community giving.

Official / primary links:

- GRiZ: https://www.mynameisgriz.com/
- GRiZ merch / official store: https://shop.mynameisgriz.com/
- GRiZ Bandcamp: https://mynameisgriz.bandcamp.com/

### GRiZMAS / Detroit giving

The 12 Days of GRiZMAS is an annual charity program supporting Seven Mile and youth music/creative arts programming in Detroit. Its official site reports more than $750,000 raised historically and $203,757.63 raised in 2025.

Official link:

- 12 Days of GRiZMAS: https://www.12daysofgrizmas.com/

### Seven Mile

The GRiZMAS beneficiary is Seven Mile, which supports youth through after-school and summer programming in music and creative arts.

When a direct official Seven Mile donation/program URL is confirmed, link it here. Until then, direct visitors through the official GRiZMAS site rather than guessing a donation endpoint.

### Other causes

GRiZ has also publicly supported LGBTQ+ causes including It Gets Better. If featured, use direct official nonprofit links and frame them as examples of causes GRiZ has supported, not as RAVE Shelter partners unless formally established.

- It Gets Better Project: https://itgetsbetter.org/

## Page structure

### 1. Hero

Use the approved **Seven Star Shelters** logo.

Headline:

**SHOW LOVE. SPREAD SHELTER LOVE.**

Subhead:

> Seven Star Shelters is the next RAVE Shelter mission installment — connecting festival community, vendor deals, shelter support, and ways to amplify the good already happening around the GRiZ community.

Primary CTAs:

- **Join the Movement** → registration
- **Become a Vendor** → `register?type=rave_vendor`
- **Browse RAVE Offers** → `/marketplace?channel=rave`

Secondary festival link:

- **Visit Seven Stars** → official Seven Stars site (external)

Affiliation disclosure immediately visible near hero CTA area:

> Independent community activation by RAVE Shelter / ShelterPawtners. No official affiliation or endorsement by GRiZ, Seven Stars, 100x, promoters, venues, or charitable organizations is implied.

### 2. Why this community

Title:

**GOOD PEOPLE. BIG HEARTS. BRIGHTER TOMORROWS.**

Copy direction:

GRiZ's community has long connected music with generosity, inclusion, creativity, and giving back. Seven Stars' published Seven Pillars emphasize sharing, inclusion, curiosity, unified heart, self-expression, leaving spaces better than we found them, and making room for joyful abandon. Seven Star Shelters should feel like a natural extension of those values into animal welfare — not an attempt to appropriate or replace them.

### 3. Seven Pillars × Shelter Love

Create an original visual interpretation, clearly labeled as **our Shelter Love translation**, not official Seven Stars copy.

Suggested mapping:

- Inclusion → Every pet deserves belonging.
- Sovereign Self → Empower adopters with tools and knowledge.
- Sharing → Share savings, resources, and support.
- Leave No Trace → Care for the spaces and communities we share.
- Curiosity → Learn better ways to care, adopt, and help.
- Kula / Unified Heart → Community support becomes shelter support.
- Bunga / Wild Abandon → Celebrate loudly; love generously.

Include a link to the official Seven Pillars page.

### 4. For Ravers / Vendors / Shelters

#### For Ravers

Discover festival-relevant offers from participating vendors, save money, support shelter-minded businesses, and help create more visibility for adoptable pets and shelters.

CTA: **Shop With Purpose**

#### For Vendors

Reach a values-driven audience through the existing RAVE Shelter marketplace. Vendors can participate without a mandatory donation; businesses that later meet the defined Hero Vendor criteria can receive additional recognition.

CTA: **Become a RAVE Vendor**

Do not invent donation percentages or financial mechanics beyond already-approved Hero Vendor policy.

#### For Shelters

Connect the energy of the festival community to measurable awareness, partnerships, and future support pathways for shelters and rescues.

CTA: **Learn About ShelterPawtners**

### 5. Amplify the love

This is important and should be visually distinct from our own CTAs.

Title:

**THE LOVE WAS ALREADY HERE. HELP IT TRAVEL FURTHER.**

Cards / links:

- **12 Days of GRiZMAS** — learn about and support the annual Detroit community-giving program.
- **Seven Mile** — describe the youth music/creative-arts beneficiary using verified language; route through official GRiZMAS source until a direct official link is verified.
- **It Gets Better Project** — optional card noting GRiZ's historic support of LGBTQ+ youth and linking directly to the nonprofit.
- **GRiZ Official** — official site / music / community links.
- **Seven Stars** — official festival information and Seven Pillars.

Do not collect donations on behalf of any of these organizations without a formal relationship and explicit implementation decision.

### 6. How Seven Star Shelters works

1. **Join** — sign up as a raver, Guardian, shelter, or vendor.
2. **Shop or Share** — discover participating offers or share the mission.
3. **Support Shelter Pets** — direct community attention and future eligible impact toward animals in need.

## Design direction

Use the approved generated concept as direction, but intentionally remove obvious AI artifacts and generic fantasy-festival clutter.

### Keep

- retro 1970s / cosmic-campout typography;
- warm gold + coral + deep night-sky palette;
- seven-star celestial motif;
- mountain/valley/campout energy;
- hearts + restrained paw motifs;
- strong readable content hierarchy;
- genuine community warmth.

### Reduce / remove

- random signs with invented slogans everywhere;
- fake festival stages, ferris wheels, or props presented as real Seven Stars locations;
- uncanny AI-generated crowds/faces;
- excessive neon haze;
- AI-looking text baked into background artwork;
- any imagery that could be mistaken for official Seven Stars photography.

### Add intentionally

- clean star fields, abstract mountain silhouettes, sunrise/sunset gradients;
- saxophone / funk / wave motifs that reference GRiZ's musical energy without using his likeness as the primary visual identity;
- verified external cards with real direct links;
- small official-site thumbnails only where usage is appropriate and sourced, otherwise use original artwork;
- simple photography placeholders if licensed/approved imagery is not yet available.

## Implementation recommendation

Do **not** create a standalone unmanaged HTML page. Build this as a normal React route inside the existing Vite application so navigation, analytics, auth, accessibility, responsive behavior, and deployment stay inside one stack.

Recommended route:

`/sevenstars`

Recommended initial files:

- `src/components/SevenStarSheltersPage.tsx`
- `src/seven-star-shelters.css`
- route registration in `src/main.tsx`
- optional original image assets under `public/brand/seven-star-shelters/`

Reuse shared RAVE Shelter registration and marketplace flows. Do not create a separate commerce implementation.

## MVP acceptance

The first production version can ship before every visual asset is final if it has:

- correct Seven Star Shelters branding;
- clear mission + no-affiliation disclosure;
- early registration/vendor CTAs;
- RAVE marketplace link;
- verified GRiZ / Seven Stars / GRiZMAS links;
- responsive desktop/mobile layouts;
- accessibility and readable contrast;
- no invented charity, donation, partnership, or endorsement claims.

Visual polish can continue after the page is live.
