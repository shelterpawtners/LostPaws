## 1. Lost Lands 2026 event activation draft

### Working event title

**Lost Lands x RAVE Shelter — LostPaws Mission Meetup**

Alternative safer/non-affiliation title if preferred:

**LostPaws at Lost Lands — RAVE Shelter Community Mission**

### Short description

**Rave with purpose. Shop with impact. Help shelter pets.**

LostPaws is bringing the RAVE Shelter mission into the Lost Lands community to connect ravers, independent vendors, creators, and shelter supporters around one simple idea: make the festival community more useful to shelter pets long after the weekend ends.

We are here to meet people, discover the vendors ravers already love, and invite those vendors to create real RAVE Shelter offers and discounts that can continue beyond Lost Lands. The goal is not a one-weekend promotion. It is to build a lasting network of favorite rave vendors, useful deals, and a community that helps amplify the ShelterPawtners mission.

### Why we are there

Lost Lands brings together people who already understand community, creativity, loyalty, and looking out for one another. LostPaws gives that energy a practical mission:

- help ravers discover useful deals from vendors they already support;
- help independent vendors reach a values-driven community;
- build long-term RAVE Shelter offers that can continue after the festival;
- introduce more people to ShelterPawtners and the broader shelter-pet mission;
- turn one conversation, one scan, or one vendor introduction into a relationship that can keep creating value after the weekend.

### What we are promoting

**For ravers:** discover RAVE Shelter vendors, current offers, and future discounts while supporting a community built around shelter pets.

**For vendors:** create a free RAVE Shelter vendor presence and publish an official offer or discount for the community. Offers can be festival-specific, ongoing, or both, subject to the normal marketplace terms and actual supported functionality.

**For shelter supporters:** help us recruit the businesses and creators the community already loves so the network becomes more useful with every new participant.

### The LostPaws vendor challenge

**Find one vendor you love. Show them the LostPaws QR code. Ask them to join RAVE Shelter.**

Suggested raver script:

> "I love what you make. Scan this — RAVE Shelter is building a marketplace for rave vendors and shelter-pet supporters. You can create a free vendor profile and publish an offer for the community that can keep going after Lost Lands."

Suggested vendor CTA:

**Scan. Join. Create an offer. Stay connected after the festival.**

The QR should always resolve to:

`https://shelterpawtners.com/lostpaws`

The page should route vendors quickly into the existing RAVE vendor registration/onboarding path and ravers into the RAVE-filtered marketplace.

### Find us

**Look for the Lucky Shamrock + Dog Paw totem.**

Come say hello, meet the people behind LostPaws, make a new friend, and become part of a community with a mission to help shelter pets while discovering deals from favorite rave vendors.

Possible CTA block:

**SEE THE SHAMROCK. FIND THE PAW. SAY HELLO.**

Then:

**Scan. Save. Share. Recruit one vendor. Help grow the mission.**

### Impact-driven takeaways

- One scan can create a new community member.
- One vendor introduction can create a lasting offer.
- One lasting offer can create value well beyond one festival weekend.
- More participating vendors make RAVE Shelter more useful to ravers and more visible to the shelter-pet mission.

### Non-affiliation language

LostPaws is an independent ShelterPawtners / RAVE Shelter initiative created by pet lovers in the festival community. LostPaws and ShelterPawtners are not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or an official program of Lost Lands, Excision, or their affiliates.
