# Launch resource research — adopted-pet benefits

Status: research seed list; every listing requires a final verification immediately before publication.

The launch Marketplace should distinguish public third-party adoption benefits from ShelterPawtners partner offers. A public listing is informational visibility, not an endorsement or partnership claim.

## Initial verified/current candidates

### PetSmart Adoption Kit

- Type: public program / adopter benefit.
- Current evidence: PetSmart's current coupon policy still references Adoption Kit coupons; PetSmart Charities' adopter FAQ states adopters still receive a free kit including a free bag of food and more than $300 in savings.
- Eligibility: tied to eligible adoptions/adoption process; exact redemption rules should be linked from PetSmart.
- Launch treatment: public_program; verify current kit details and destination URL immediately before publish.

### Adopt a Pet Shelter Plus

- Type: public program / adopter benefit + shelter program.
- Current evidence: 2026 Shelter Plus site states adopters from participating Shelter Plus shelters receive guidance and special discounts; Adopt a Pet's February 2026 program article states adopters receive a free PEDIGREE or IAMS sample bag and a coupon booklet with offers from PEDIGREE, IAMS, Nutro, Royal Canin, Greenies, Temptations, Pretty Litter, and Whistle.
- Eligibility: adoption from a participating Shelter Plus organization.
- Launch treatment: public_program.

### Trupanion Shelter & Rescue Support / Adoption Day Offers

- Type: public program / adoption insurance benefit.
- Current evidence: Trupanion's current shelter program offers eligible adopted pets 30 days of immediate coverage through participating shelters/rescues.
- Eligibility/terms vary by organization and offer type.
- Launch treatment: public_program.

### PetPartners 30-Day Pet Insurance Coverage

- Type: public program / adoption insurance benefit.
- Current evidence: PetPartners currently offers 30-day coverage for newly adopted dogs and cats with an activation code from a participating veterinarian, shelter, or rescue; no credit card is required to enroll.
- Launch treatment: public_program.

### 24Petwatch Shelter Adoption Program

- Type: public program / adoption insurance trial.
- Current evidence: current enrollment pages state the shelter adoption program provides an initial 30 days of a 12-month insurance term as a trial, subject to eligibility, and must be activated within seven days of adoption.
- Important disclosure: payment information is required and billing continues unless canceled.
- Launch treatment: public_program with prominent terms disclosure.

### Banfield Shelter Animal Veterinary Discount adopter benefit

- Type: public program / veterinary benefit.
- Current evidence: Banfield's SAVD page states that adopters from a SAVD shelter/rescue can access a Free First Exam Coupon at Banfield; the 25% SAVD discount itself is for shelters, not adopters.
- Launch treatment: public_program; wording must distinguish shelter discount from adopter exam benefit.

### BISSELL Pet Foundation — Empty the Shelters

- Type: public program / reduced adoption fee.
- Current evidence: BISSELL Pet Foundation states Empty the Shelters is held quarterly and adopters pay no more than $70 at participating shelters. The current Fall National Adoption Event is September 18–30, 2026.
- Launch treatment: public_program with date range and participating-shelter link.

### Petfinder / Purina Feeding Partners

- Type: public program / shelter benefit with adopter support.
- Current evidence: qualified Petfinder Pro shelters/rescues can save up to 50% on select Purina Pro Plan products and receive adopter-facing Purina materials; program terms allow adopter recommendations that may include samples/promotional materials.
- Launch treatment: community or public_program targeted primarily to shelters, not misrepresented as a universal consumer discount.

### Hill's Food, Shelter & Love

- Type: public program / shelter support, sometimes adopter starter bags/coupons.
- Current evidence: Hill's current program supports 1,000+ shelters with discounted nutrition. In August 2026 Hill's also distributed adopter kits at select shelters with Science Diet starter bags, coupons, and supplies.
- Launch treatment: community/public_program for the ongoing shelter program; seasonal adopter promotions should carry exact date windows and expire automatically.

### GoodPup shelter programs

- Type: shelter partnership program / adopter training discount.
- Current evidence: GoodPup documents a Shelter Affiliate Program allowing participating shelters to give adopters lifetime training discounts and earn donations. The partner inquiry program remains live.
- Launch treatment: research candidate; re-confirm exact current adopter terms before public listing because the detailed discount description is older than the other launch candidates.

### MetLife Pet shelter/rescue programs

- Type: shelter program / pet insurance benefit.
- Current evidence: MetLife currently works with shelter/rescue organizations and may be offered to adopters; 2026 shelter examples show complimentary 30-day coverage in participating programs.
- Launch treatment: public_program only where a current adopter-facing program or participating-shelter source supports the exact claim.

### Petco / Petco Love

- Type: adoption/resource ecosystem.
- Current evidence: Petco Love currently supports 4,000+ animal welfare organizations and advertises adoption support benefits through participating Petco locations. Petco's 2024 Welcome to the Family coupon booklet had savings through November 2025; those specific coupon values are expired and must not be presented as current in 2026 without new confirmation.
- Launch treatment: community/public adoption resource unless a current 2026 discount is independently verified.

### Zoetis + Adopt a Pet adopter resources

- Type: free adopter resources.
- Current evidence: Zoetis/Adopt a Pet currently offer a free Pet Adopter Starter Pack with health/checklist resources.
- Launch treatment: community resource, not a discount unless a monetary offer is separately verified.
