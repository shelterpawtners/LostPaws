# Marketplace research and savings strategy

## Purpose and status

This document records the initial research basis and product strategy for the ShelterPawtners savings marketplace. It supports roadmap planning and does not establish a partnership, endorsement, affiliate relationship, or guaranteed offer.

The marketplace should create useful guardian value before ShelterPawtners has negotiated private deals. It can begin with carefully curated public savings, then add exclusive partner offers and verified-adoption benefits as real agreements and eligibility controls become available.

Offer terms change frequently. Every displayed offer needs a direct source, eligibility summary, geographic scope when relevant, last-verified date, and a clear path to the provider's current terms. Do not describe a public offer as a ShelterPawtners benefit or exclusive deal.

## Marketplace value proposition

The initial marketplace should save guardians time as well as money. Its first useful job is to gather scattered programs into a trustworthy, searchable experience that explains:

- what the savings opportunity is;
- who provides it;
- whether it is public, ShelterPawtners exclusive, or restricted to verified adopters;
- whether enrollment, subscription, membership, prescription, location, or purchase history is required;
- when the information was last checked;
- where the current provider terms can be reviewed.

The marketplace is not a coupon scraper. Quality, clarity, relevance, freshness, and trust are more valuable than a large count of stale links.

## Offer classification

| Label                     | Meaning                                                               | MVP treatment                                               |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Public offer              | A provider publishes the offer for eligible members of the public     | Allowed after source and terms review                       |
| Public program            | An ongoing loyalty, autoship, rebate, comparison, or discount program | Allowed with program requirements and last-verified date    |
| ShelterPawtners exclusive | A benefit created through a confirmed ShelterPawtners agreement       | Do not display until the agreement and terms are documented |
| Verified-adoption benefit | A benefit restricted to qualifying adopted pets                       | Do not unlock based only on guardian self-assertion         |
| Sponsored placement       | Paid or otherwise compensated visibility                              | Clearly disclose sponsorship and preserve relevance         |
| Affiliate link            | ShelterPawtners may receive compensation from a tracked referral      | Clearly disclose before use and follow provider terms       |
| Community or event offer  | A benefit tied to a real participating campaign, event, or vendor     | Display only with confirmed authorization, dates, and scope |

## Marketplace channels

Use a shared offer foundation with distinct channels:

- **ShelterPawtners marketplace:** pet products, pet services, care, medication, adoption support, and guardian savings.
- **LostPaws / Rave Shelter marketplace:** human apparel, accessories, art, food, wellness, festival preparation, music-community services, and other lawful attendee-focused offers.
- **Shared or community:** offers that legitimately serve more than one audience.

Rave Shelter partners may self-publish during the MVP. The platform must preserve organization attribution, audience and channel selection, terms, timestamps, reporting, edit and unpublish capability, team monitoring, and suspension controls. Registration or publication does not mean the partner is verified or officially affiliated with a named festival.

See [Festival MVP and adoption verification](FESTIVAL-MVP-AND-VERIFICATION.md).

## MVP guardian experience

After account creation or login, the guardian home should present two useful paths:

1. **Set up your pet**, shown as the recommended primary action.
2. **Browse savings**, available immediately so a new user receives value even before completing a Passport.

Pet setup should improve marketplace relevance over time, but the MVP should not require unnecessary pet or guardian data merely to view generally available offers. Initial personalization can use explicit, low-risk choices such as species, broad location, and selected interests after the related privacy decisions are approved.

Recommended marketplace capabilities for MVP:

- curated offer and program cards;
- categories for food and supplies, medication, veterinary care, grooming, training, boarding and daycare, insurance or financial support, new-pet offers, and local services;
- keyword search and practical filters;
- provider identity, offer classification, eligibility, expiration or ongoing status, source link, and last-verified date;
- saved offers or outbound-click tracking only after measurement and consent choices are defined;
- report-an-issue feedback;
- accessible empty, expired, unavailable, and loading states;
- internal curation workflow with publish, unpublish, verify, and expire controls.

## Curation and freshness standard

A public savings catalog needs an operational process from launch:

1. Research from the provider's official page whenever possible.
2. Record the provider, source URL, classification, summary, important conditions, geography, start and end dates when known, and verification timestamp.
3. Review language for accuracy and avoid copying promotional text unnecessarily.
4. Require a human publication decision for curated third-party public offers. Authenticated partner listings may self-publish under the separate monitored MVP rules.
5. Recheck time-limited offers before their displayed expiration and ongoing programs on a defined cadence.
6. Unpublish broken, expired, materially changed, or unverifiable entries.
7. Preserve revision history for material offer changes.
8. Provide a correction channel for providers and users.

Automated discovery may assist later, but it must not auto-publish claims. Provider feeds, affiliate networks, and direct partner submissions can improve scale after their terms, disclosures, and quality controls are approved.

## Research expansion backlog

Build a broader catalog systematically rather than promising exhaustive coverage. Research should expand across:

- national retailers and pharmacies;
- pet food and supply subscriptions;
- veterinary networks and wellness plans;
- medication manufacturers and rebate centers;
- insurers, discount plans, financing, and charitable assistance;
- grooming, training, walking, sitting, boarding, and daycare;
- identification, microchip, recovery, transportation, and travel;
- local Michigan businesses for the first geographic launch;
- shelter- and rescue-provided adoption packages;
- employer, association, military, senior, student, and community eligibility programs;
- festival, artist, vendor, and event offers only after real participation exists.

For each category, compare actual guardian value, coverage, restrictions, ease of redemption, data requested, program stability, and potential referral or partnership path.

## Risks and controls

- **Stale promotions:** show freshness and expiration, and remove unverified offers.
- **False affiliation:** use precise classification and never apply partner branding without permission.
- **Misleading savings:** distinguish advertised savings from realized savings and avoid unsupported totals.
- **Affiliate bias:** disclose compensation and do not let payment determine relevance without labeling.
- **Unsafe health framing:** medication and veterinary entries link to provider terms and do not replace veterinary advice.
- **Privacy overcollection:** allow general browsing without requiring a complete Passport.
- **Eligibility failures:** enforce private or adoption-only offers on the server when those capabilities ship.
- **Content ownership:** summarize facts and link to sources rather than copying proprietary promotional content.
