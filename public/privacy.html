Privacy Policy

We built ShelterPawtners around trust, practical value, and responsible stewardship of information about people, pets, shelters, and participating businesses.

Effective: September 12, 2026Last updated: September 12, 2026
This policy describes the current ShelterPawtners, RAVE Shelter, Marketplace, Digital Pet Passport, adoption-support, and related community experiences. Features may evolve, and we will update this policy when our practices materially change.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use ShelterPawtners, we may collect or process:

  • account and authentication information, such as your name, email address, login provider, and account role;
  • organization information for shelters, rescues, pet businesses, providers, vendors, and other partners;
  • pet and Digital Pet Passport information that you or an authorized participant provide, including profile details, photos, adoption history, and care-related records;
  • adoption-verification information, such as shelter contact information, adoption details, verification status, supporting records, and security metadata used to complete the verification process;
  • Marketplace and offer activity, such as offers viewed, claimed, redeemed, or otherwise used;
  • messages, support requests, bug reports, suggestions, and other information you submit to us; and
  • technical, browser, device, security, diagnostic, and log information reasonably needed to operate, protect, and improve the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure accounts, operate Digital Pet Passports, support adoption verification, provide Marketplace and RAVE Shelter experiences, administer offers, support users and partners, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, improve reliability, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We may also use aggregated or de-identified information to understand how the platform is used, improve services, and develop insights that support shelters and the broader animal-welfare mission. We do not intend for those uses to expose a Guardian's personally identifiable information.

3. When we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us host, authenticate, communicate, secure, and operate the platform; with shelters, rescues, businesses, providers, or other participants when you request or authorize a workflow that requires the disclosure; when required by law; or when reasonably necessary to protect people, animals, users, rights, or the service.

Current or planned service providers may include infrastructure and authentication providers such as Supabase and Vercel, identity providers such as Google and Meta when enabled, and communications providers used to operate ShelterPawtners. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices as applicable.

4. Authentication and third-party login

ShelterPawtners uses account authentication services and may offer sign-in through third-party providers such as Google or Facebook. When you choose a third-party login, that provider may send us information needed to authenticate your account, such as your provider identifier, name, or email address, depending on the permissions you grant.

5. Digital Pet Passport privacy

Digital Pet Passports are intended to be Guardian-controlled. Private information should remain private unless you deliberately share it or a feature you choose requires a limited disclosure. Public or emergency-facing experiences are designed to avoid exposing unrelated private account information or internal identifiers.

6. Cookies, local storage, and similar technology

We may use browser storage, session storage, authentication storage, cookies, or similar technologies that are necessary to keep you signed in, remember preferences, protect the service, and operate features. If we later add analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies that require additional notice or consent, we will update this policy and the product experience as appropriate.

7. Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain appropriate pet-history continuity, provide support, protect security and integrity, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention can vary by record type and feature.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the current product, including authentication controls and database access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use strong account credentials and contact us if you believe your account or information may have been compromised.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a copy of personal information. You may also ask us questions about our privacy practices. We will verify requests as reasonably necessary and respond consistent with applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping obligations.

To make a privacy request, email contact@shelterpawtners.com with the subject line Privacy Request.

10. Children

ShelterPawtners is not designed as a child-directed service. If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that is not permitted by applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

11. Sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. We may create or use aggregate or de-identified information for analytics, research, product improvement, and mission-related insights where appropriate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ShelterPawtners evolves. When changes are material, we will update the effective date and provide additional notice when appropriate.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact ShelterPawtners at contact@shelterpawtners.com.
